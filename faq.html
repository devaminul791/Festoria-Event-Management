Frequently Asked Questions

Colour palettes, what a fair décor quote looks like, and the small things our stylists wish every host knew before the big day.

How far ahead should I book?

Two to four weeks for most parties. Saturdays from April to October go fast, and weddings are usually booked six to nine months ahead.

Can I choose my own colours?

Yes. Send us your invitation, a swatch or a photo and we'll match it. We stock more than 80 balloon colours, including chrome, pastel and matte finishes.

What does the package price include?

The design, all balloons and materials, delivery, setup and teardown within 25 miles of Atlanta. Rentals, florals and late-night teardown are listed separately on your proposal.

Do you work with my venue?

Almost certainly. We are on the approved vendor list of more than 60 Atlanta venues and we send our insurance certificate to any venue that asks.

Do you need a deposit?

Parties need a $100 deposit to hold the date, taken off your final bill. Weddings are paid 30% at booking and the rest two weeks before.

Can I get a quote without a consultation?

Yes. Send us the venue, the date and a few photos of the look you love. For garlands and party setups that is usually enough.

What happens if it rains on the day?

Outdoor balloons are built to handle a light breeze, but if storms are forecast we move the installation indoors or reschedule it free of charge.

Do you rent décor without styling?

Yes. Arches, plinths, backdrops, neon signs and linens can be rented for pickup from our warehouse, with delivery available for a small fee.
