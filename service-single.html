Balloon Garlands & Arches

  1. Home
  2. Services
  3. Balloon Garlands & Arches
Featured

Our signature service: organic balloon garlands and arches designed around your colour story and built on site by our stylists.

Balloon garlands are where Festoria began, and they are still what we are asked for most. Every garland is hand-built from mixed sizes so it looks full and organic, never like a row of identical circles. We build on site, so nothing is crushed on the way and the shape fits your wall, doorway or table exactly.

What’s Included:

  • A colour consultation with swatches or photos of your invitation
  • An organic garland from 6 to 30 ft, or a free-standing arch up to 10 ft wide
  • Balloons from 5 to 24 inches in up to five colours
  • Optional greenery, dried florals or metallic accents
  • Delivery, setup, weights and teardown

Why Clients Choose It:

  • Designs matched to your theme, not a fixed catalogue
  • Long-lasting, biodegradable latex balloons
  • Indoor and outdoor builds rated for light wind
  • Photos of the finished install sent to you

How It Works: Tell us your date, venue and colours, and send a photo of the space. We reply with a sketch and a fixed price within one business day. On the day, two stylists arrive two to three hours before your guests and build everything in place.

Who It’s For: Birthdays, baby showers, gender reveals, graduations, store openings and anyone who wants the photo wall everyone talks about.

What's Included

  • Colour consultation
  • Organic garland or full arch
  • Mixed sizes from 5 to 24 in
  • Delivery, setup & teardown
  • Weights, frames & fixings

Key Benefits

  • More than 80 balloon colours in stock
  • Matte, pastel, chrome & clear finishes
  • Built on site, never pre-squashed in a van
  • Venue-safe fixings, no tape on walls
  • Free replacement if one pops before the party
